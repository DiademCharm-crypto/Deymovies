Questions

Is this a subscription?

No. It's a one-time tip. Nothing renews, nothing is charged automatically, and no account is created.

What do I get for donating?

Nothing extra — and by design. Every title stays free for everyone. Your tip pays for bandwidth and hosting, so the site keeps running.

Why does my app show a different account name?

The wallet is registered under the owner's legal name, which is what any bank or e-wallet is required to display for an account transfer. It isn't a business name, so make sure the number matches the one above before you send.

Will you ever ask for my MPIN or OTP?

Never. A real donation takes one transfer from your own app. Anyone asking for your MPIN, OTP, or card details is impersonating the site — stop and report it.

Can I get a refund?

InstaPay transfers are final and cannot be reversed, so please double-check the number and amount before you confirm.
